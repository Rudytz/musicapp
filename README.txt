Put your mp3 files in this folder with these exact names:

songs/gods-plan.mp3  (Drake - God's Plan)
songs/one-dance.mp3  (Drake - One Dance)
songs/hotline-bling.mp3  (Drake - Hotline Bling)
songs/passionfruit.mp3  (Drake - Passionfruit)
songs/come-and-see-me.mp3  (PARTYNEXTDOOR - Come and See Me)
songs/recognize.mp3  (PARTYNEXTDOOR - Recognize)
songs/persian-rugs.mp3  (PARTYNEXTDOOR - Persian Rugs)
songs/no-idea.mp3  (Don Toliver - No Idea)
songs/after-party.mp3  (Don Toliver - After Party)
songs/cardigan.mp3  (Don Toliver - Cardigan)

To add another song, copy a line in the songs list at the top of script.js and change the title, artist, mood, and file name.
